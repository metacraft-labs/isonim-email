Lists and tables
================

Lists nested three levels deep, a loose list, and tables whose columns are 
aligned left, centre and right.

Before you upgrade
------------------

1. Back up the database
   - with acme backup, or
   - from the dashboard:
     1. open Settings
     2. choose Backups
2. Read the release notes (https://example.com/notes)
3. Upgrade

- Tight items hold their text directly.
  - A nested list sits under its item.

Loose items keep their paragraphs:

- The first item's paragraph.
  - and its sub-list, after a blank line.
- The second item.
